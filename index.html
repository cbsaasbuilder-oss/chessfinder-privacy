ChessFinder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

ChessFinder ("the app") is an Android app that films a chess board shown on a screen, recognises the position and shows chess engine suggestions on top of the camera image. It is developed by CBDev ("we", "us").

The short version:

  • Your camera images never leave your phone. The board, the pieces and the engine analysis are all computed on the device.
  • There is no account, no advertising and no tracking for advertising.
  • Crash reports and anonymous usage statistics are optional. The app asks you once, at first launch, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Support.
  • Feedback is only sent when you write it and press Send.

1. What stays on your phone

Data What happens to it
Camera images Processed live in memory to find the board and the pieces. They are not stored and never sent anywhere.
Board positions, moves and engine lines Computed on the device by the built-in Stockfish engine. Never sent to us.
Settings (lines, depth, colours...) and your privacy choices Stored in the app's private storage on the phone.
Last crash report (text) Stored in the app's private storage so that you can choose to share it from the settings.
Training squares (only if you turn on Collect training squares) Small images of board squares, saved on the phone. They leave the phone only if you share them yourself with Share squares, through Android's share menu, to the app or person you pick.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. Android backup is turned off for the app, so none of it is copied to your Google account backup.

2. What is sent, and only with your agreement

2.1 Crash reports (optional)

If you allow crash reports, the app sends error reports to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), stored in Sentry's EU region, when the app crashes, freezes or hits an error it can recover from.

A crash report contains: the error and the program's stack trace, the app version, the Android version, the phone model and similar technical details (memory, screen size, language), and a random identifier for the installation. It does not contain screenshots, camera images, chess positions, your IP address (the app tells Sentry not to collect it) or any account or name.

Purpose: finding and fixing bugs. Legal basis (GDPR): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

2.2 Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

If you allow usage statistics, the app sends anonymous events to PostHog (PostHog Inc.), hosted on PostHog Cloud EU (Frankfurt, Germany). Examples: the app was opened, an analysis session started or ended and how long it lasted, how fast the board was found, the frame rate, how long the engine took, which settings are used, that the Pro screen was shown or Pro was bought, that the sharing menu was used.

Events carry counts, timings and settings only, with a random identifier for the installation. They do not contain camera images, chess positions or moves, your name, email or advertising ID. No user profiles are built, no location is derived from your IP address, and IP addresses are discarded. Turning statistics off deletes the random identifier on the phone; turning them back on starts with a new one.

Purpose: understanding which features are used and how well detection works on different phones, to improve the app. Legal basis (GDPR): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

2.3 Feedback (only when you send it)

Settings → Support → Send feedback sends the message you type to us through Sentry (EU region), with the app version and phone model. Please do not write personal information in it. This works even if crash reports are off, because you chose to send the message.

Purpose: reading and answering your feedback. Legal basis (GDPR): your request (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3. Purchases (ChessFinder Pro)

ChessFinder Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google: we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether Pro was bought on your Google account and keeps that answer on the phone so Pro works offline. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. If usage statistics are on, the anonymous "Pro bought" event described in section 2.2 is sent (no price, order number or account).

4. Things you choose to do outside the app

  • Share PGN / copy FEN: the moves or position go only where you send or paste them.
  • Open in an online analysis board: the position is opened in your web browser on a third-party website. That website then receives the position and the usual information any website receives, under its own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: to film the screen showing the board. Images are processed on the phone only.
  • Internet / network state: only for the optional crash reports, usage statistics and feedback above, and for purchases through Google Play.

6. Who receives data

We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data is processed only by the service providers above, on our behalf:

Provider Role Location
Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) Crash reports and feedback EU region
PostHog Inc. Anonymous usage statistics EU (Frankfurt)
Google (Google Play) App distribution and purchases Google's own policy applies

Both providers act as processors under data processing agreements and may use sub-processors listed in their own documentation. All data is sent encrypted (HTTPS); the app blocks unencrypted connections.

7. How long data is kept

  • Crash reports and feedback: 90 days, then deleted automatically by Sentry.
  • Usage statistics: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Data on the phone: until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw your consent at any time (in Settings → Support, or by uninstalling the app). Withdrawing consent does not affect what was sent before.

Because we have no account and no name or email linked to the data, we usually cannot tell which reports come from you. If you want us to find and delete them, write to us and we will do our best, for example using the time and phone model of a report.

You can also complain to your data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

9. Children

ChessFinder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

10. Open-source software

ChessFinder includes Stockfish, a free chess engine licensed under the GNU General Public License v3. See Settings → Support → Licenses in the app for the notice and where to get its source code.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. If a change affects what is sent, the app will ask for your consent again.
